How these numbers are worked out
  • Sale price is the lowest of the region's average sale price, this realm's market value and its cheapest listing: what an item really sells for, not the highest listing.
  • Materials cost this realm's cheapest listing, or an Alliance vendor's price. The cheapest listing usually covers only a few units, so bulk buying costs a little more.
  • TSM counts ammo per shot, so 18,000 Heavy Shot a day at 2 copper each is only a few gold of trade.
  • Cooldown crafts (Mooncloth, Arcanite, Salt Shaker) are limited per character, so one crafter takes a fixed share of the pool however many sell.
  • Skill levels are what a trainer teaches the recipe at. The recipe data gets many of these wrong on Forever, so skills read off trainers in game replace it; a ~ means an estimate from the recipe's colours that hasn't been checked at a trainer yet.
  • Gathered items count their whole sale value, since the cost is your time. Looted gear counts what the AH pays over the vendor price.
  • Realm prices refresh about hourly and region sales about daily. In-game readings are added whenever the auction house was opened and the game saved its data.
